# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Resonance serves music students, music-teacher-education students, and their
teachers at a German university of music. Students often work in practice rooms
or school placements with unreliable connectivity. Teachers commonly review
batches of evidence at a desk on iPad with a keyboard.

## Product Purpose

Resonance is an offline-first practice evidence and feedback tool. Students set
a goal, capture audio or consented teaching-lesson video, submit it privately to
a course, and receive structured timestamped feedback. Success means that every
person can tell what is stored locally, queued, uploaded, submitted, or reviewed
and can recover safely from poor connectivity or an expired session.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and precise. Resonance should feel like a quiet studio tool
that keeps attention on musical work and teaching reflection.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a consumer social app, analytics dashboard, engineering console,
generic academic portal, or decorative AI-generated interface. Avoid gradients,
blurred decorative orbs, glass cards, glow, broad shadows, bounce animation,
placeholder emblems, generic templates, and card-per-section layouts.

## Design Principles

1. Put evidence and the current task before decoration.
2. Make local, remote, queued, failed, and reviewed state explicit in words.
3. Treat account ownership, course role, consent, and private media as hard boundaries.
4. Make one user action produce one durable and recoverable outcome.
5. Prefer familiar adaptive platform behavior over custom interaction vocabulary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA-equivalent native behavior and support VoiceOver, Voice
Control, Switch Control, Full Keyboard Access, Reduce Motion, Reduce
Transparency, Increase Contrast, Bold Text, and AX1 through AX5 Dynamic Type.
The production-pilot target is German as the primary locale with a complete English fallback; localization resources are not yet implemented. Core student
capture and recovery flows must be validated on iPhone; the full product must be validated on
iPad in portrait, landscape, and resizable windows.
